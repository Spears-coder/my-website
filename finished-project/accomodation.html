Semonkong Lodge

Semonkong Lodge is a breathtaking riverside retreat in the Maluti Mountains. Stay in cozy thatched rondavels, experience the world’s highest commercial abseil beside Maletsunyane Falls, ride traditional Basotho ponies, and unwind by the fire at the lively Duck and Donkey Tavern.

Avani Lesotho

Avani Lesotho Hotel & Casino is a premier destination in Maseru combining luxury and excitement. Enjoy a sparkling outdoor pool, an on-site casino, great dining, and stunning valley views, making it the ultimate relaxing base for your Lesotho adventure.

Maliba Lodge

Maliba Lodge is a luxury five-star retreat nestled in the pristine Ts'ehlanyane National Park. Surrounded by rugged mountains and crystal-clear streams, it offers an exclusive highland escape combining high-end comfort with untouched natural beauty.

Malealea Lodge

Malealea Lodge: A legendary, rustic eco-lodge located in the remote western mountains. It is famous for its breathtaking scenery, pony trekking adventures, and evening performances by local Basotho choir and string bands.

Sani Mountain Lodge

Sani Mountain Lodge (Sani Mountain Escape): Perched high on the escarpment at the top of the dramatic Sani Pass, it features cozy stone rondavels and is home to the highest pub in Africa.
